John MooseBand

Alternative rock / folk · Sweden

Through our work with the cultural cooperative John Moose/Anlesser—focusing on audiovisual and digital productions—we have gained recognition both nationally and internationally, receiving awards such as the Guldägget (Golden Egg) and the Guldvargen (Golden Wolf). We were also honored with the title "Värmland Cooperative of the Year 2016."
